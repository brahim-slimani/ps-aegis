# Order Fulfilment SOP

## Order lifecycle

An order moves through the statuses pending, processing, shipped and delivered. Pending orders have been received but payment is not yet confirmed. Processing orders are being picked and packed in the warehouse.

## Cancellations

Customers may cancel an order free of charge while it is pending or processing. Once an order is shipped it can no longer be cancelled; the customer must use the returns process within 14 days of delivery. Every cancellation must be confirmed by a support agent or manager.

## Refunds

Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5 to 7 working days after a cancellation or an approved return. Orders above 1,000 EUR need manager approval before the refund is released.

## Delayed shipments

If a shipment is delayed by more than 3 working days, support must proactively contact the customer and offer a 10% discount code on the next order.
