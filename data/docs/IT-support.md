# IT Support Handbook

## Ticket priorities

Tickets are triaged into four priorities. Urgent tickets (production outage, security incident) get a first response within 30 minutes, 24/7. High priority tickets are answered within 2 hours, medium within 1 working day, and low within 3 working days.

## Password reset

Employees can reset their password at the self-service portal. If MFA is locked, open a ticket with the category "access" and attach a photo of your company badge. The service desk verifies your identity by calling your manager before resetting MFA.

## Hardware requests

Laptops are renewed every 36 months. Request a replacement earlier only if the device is damaged or fails the diagnostics tool. Hardware requests above 1,500 EUR require approval from the department head.

## VPN

The corporate VPN is mandatory when accessing internal systems from outside the office. Install the client from the software portal and sign in with your company account. Split tunnelling is disabled.
