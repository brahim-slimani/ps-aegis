# Leave Policy

## Annual leave

Every full-time employee receives 25 days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Leave accrues monthly and up to 5 unused days can be carried over to the next year until 31 March.

Requests must be submitted in the HR portal at least two weeks in advance for absences longer than five consecutive days. Managers approve or reject requests within three working days.

## Sick leave

Employees who are ill must notify their manager before 9:00 on the first day of absence. A medical certificate is required from the third consecutive day of sickness. Sick leave is paid at 100% for the first 30 days.

## Parental leave

Birth parents receive 16 weeks of fully paid parental leave. Non-birth parents receive 8 weeks. Leave may be taken in up to two blocks within 12 months of the birth or adoption.
